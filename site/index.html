What Attn does

Mail stays on your computer
Your mail is in a SQLite file on your disk, so Attn works offline. There is no server and no telemetry.
Fast from the keyboard
Archive, snooze, reply, and undo with one key each. The command palette finds every other action.
AI with your own key
Draft a reply, complete a sentence, or describe an inbox split in plain words. AI is off until you add a key.
The rest of a mail client
Several Gmail accounts, inbox splits, snooze, follow-up reminders, and undo send.
