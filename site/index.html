What Attn does

Mail stays on your computer
Your mail is in a SQLite file on your disk. Attn has no server and no telemetry.
Works without a connection
Read, write, and sort mail offline. Attn sends the changes to Gmail when you are back online.
Several Gmail accounts
Each account has a separate inbox, and all of them continue to sync and send.
Splits, snooze, and reminders
Sort the inbox into tabs with rules, send mail away until later, and get a reminder if nobody replies.
