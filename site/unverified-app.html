Attn

The Google sign-in warning

When you sign in to a release build of Attn, Google shows a page that says "Google hasn't verified this app". This page explains the warning, so that you can decide if you continue.

Why Google shows it

Google reviews each application that reads Gmail. The review takes weeks, and Attn has not completed it. Until it does, Google warns every person who signs in. The warning is correct: Google has not checked Attn. You can check it yourself, because the source code is public.

Check that you have the real Attn

A fake copy of Attn can show you the same warning. Do these checks before you continue:

  • Install Attn only from the GitHub releases page or from this site.
  • Make sure that the Google page names the application as Attn.
  • Make sure that the sign-in page is on accounts.google.com.

How to continue

  1. On the warning page, select Advanced.
  2. Select Go to Attn (unsafe).
  3. Read the permissions that Google lists, then select Continue.

What the permission allows

Attn asks to read, write, send, and organize your Gmail. A mail client needs all of these. Attn keeps your mail on your computer and has no server. The privacy policy lists every case where data leaves your computer.

If you cannot continue

Google does not offer the Advanced link in these cases:

  • Your Google Workspace administrator blocks unverified applications.
  • Your account uses the Advanced Protection Program.
  • Attn has reached Google's limit of 100 users for an unverified application.

In each case, you can use Attn with your own Google OAuth client. Follow the steps in the README.

How to remove access

Open your Google Account permissions, select Attn, and remove its access. Attn can no longer read your mail after that.
